An app by ChinaRetailDesign

China, made navigable before you fly

A preparation guide for foreign visitors to mainland China: what to arrange, which apps to install and how to set them up, how eSIMs really behave, and what each city expects from you on arrival.

Works fully offline No account No tracking Free

Everything to sort out before the trip

Not a guidebook — a pre-departure checklist with the reasoning attached. Each step explains why it matters and what happens if you skip it.

1

Preparation checklist

Visa-free entry windows, cash to carry, what must be installed before boarding, and what can only be done after landing.

2

Payments, step by step

Alipay and WeChat Pay setup written for first-time users: card limits, ID checks, and the two settings that catch almost everyone.

3

The apps that matter

Maps, metro, trains, translation and delivery — which ones work in English, which need a Chinese number, and where each is required.

4

City essentials

Per-city notes on transport cards, airport transfers, neighbourhoods, and the payment quirks of each place.

5

eSIM, explained honestly

Roaming vs China-market eSIMs, real coverage differences behind the firewall, and published tariff figures — with no carrier recommended.

6

Phrasebook and tools

Essential phrases with pinyin, a currency converter, and a one-tap local data wipe. All stored and computed on your device only.
