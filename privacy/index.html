Privacy Policy

China Travel Assistant · Last updated: 2026-10-15

Summary

China Travel Assistant does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. What data this app collects

None. We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit: your name, email address, phone number or any identifier; your location or GPS coordinates; your contacts, photos or files; your browsing history or search queries; your IP address as an end user (no server receives it, because no server exists); or any personal or sensitive information of any kind.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following values locally only, in your device's WebView storage, and never transmits them anywhere:

DataWhy it is storedWhere it is stored
Saved city listSo your starred cities survive an app restartOn-device local storage
Checklist progress ticksSo your preparation progress is not lostOn-device local storage

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, not synced, and not visible to us. Because it is stored only locally, uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes it permanently and irrecoverably — we hold no copy of it anywhere.

You can delete it at any time using Tools → Your data → Delete local data, which clears both lists immediately. Clearing the app's storage from the Android system settings has the same effect.

3. Android permissions

This app does not request any of the following:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (location)
  • CAMERA (camera)
  • RECORD_AUDIO (microphone)
  • READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTS (contacts)
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (photos and files)
  • READ_PHONE_STATE (phone, IMEI, call log)

The app contains no functionality that would require them. If a future version requests a permission, this policy will be updated before release and the request will be justified in context.

4. Internet access and data transmission

The app does not request the INTERNET permission at all, so it is technically incapable of making network requests. All content — guides, phrasebook and the exchange rate table — is packaged inside the app and served from local assets. The app makes no requests to our servers, to analytics endpoints, or to advertising networks, and it does not send any information about you to any third party.

Bundled web content is loaded by the WebView from local app storage rather than over the network. Links to third-party sites open in your system browser, which is a separate app with its own network permissions and its own privacy policy.

5. Third-party services and SDKs

The app bundles no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, crash reporting, or social SDKs. It has no dependency on Google Play Services, Google Analytics, Firebase, or any advertising network.

5a. Advertising, affiliate links and commercial relationships

The current release contains:

  • no advertising of any kind, and no advertising SDK;
  • no affiliate links and no commission or referral tracking of any kind;
  • no sponsored placements and no commercial relationship with any telecom operator, eSIM provider or travel service;
  • no purchase links and no in-app selling of any product or service.

The eSIM section of the app is informational only. It lists mechanism differences, published tariff figures and documented pitfalls, and it expressly does not recommend or rank any carrier, operator or provider.

Because no third-party service receives a referral, no traffic is attributed to any partner and no such partner exists, the app has no third-party tracking of any kind. If paid sponsorship is ever introduced, this policy, the in-app disclosure and the Google Play Data Safety form will all be updated before that release ships.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

7. International transfers

None. No personal data is collected, so no data is transferred across borders.

8. Data retention

Since no data is collected, nothing is retained. Locally stored values persist only until you delete them through the app or uninstall it.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or erase. You have complete control over the data on your device:

  • delete saved cities and checklist progress: Tools → Delete local data
  • delete everything: clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall

10. Security

Data never leaves your device, so there is no transmission to secure and no server to breach. Your device's own protections — screen lock, encryption, and Android's app sandboxing — apply to the locally stored values.

11. Changes to this policy

Any material change to this policy will be published on this page alongside the release that introduced it, with the "Last updated" date changed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or this app: email sammyhuang@chinaretaildesign.com.

Disclaimer

China Travel Assistant provides general travel information for foreign visitors to mainland China. It is provided as-is, is not legal or travel advice, and should not be relied upon as a substitute for official government, embassy, airline, or operator sources. Policies described in the app change frequently — always verify details before you travel.
